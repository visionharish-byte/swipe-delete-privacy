Privacy Policy – Swipe Delete Gallery

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Swipe Delete Gallery is an Android application that helps users browse, select, share, and delete photos and videos stored on their devices.

1. Information We Access

The app accesses photos and videos as permitted by Android permissions to display media and provide its management features.

2. How Information Is Used

Media access is used to provide browsing, selection, sharing, and deletion features. The app is designed to process media locally on your device and does not require an account.

3. Sharing and Deletion

When you choose to share media, Android and the app you select handle the sharing action. Deleting or moving media to Trash may require Android confirmation. Review your selections carefully.

4. Data Collection and Disclosure

The app is designed not to transmit your photos or videos to our servers. This policy does not cover information you independently share through another app or service.

5. Data Security and Retention

Your media remains subject to your device's storage and Android permission controls. The app does not provide cloud storage for your media.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is a general-purpose media utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Revised versions will be published on this page.
